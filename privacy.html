R8K0 Guardian Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

R8K0 Guardian is a Discord bot operated by R8K0 Mods. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how that information may be used, and how users may request removal of stored information.

1. Information We May Collect

R8K0 Guardian may process or store information including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server or Guild IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Discord Message IDs
  • Server configuration settings
  • Ticket information
  • Moderation records
  • Logging configuration
  • Command usage information
  • Subscription or premium status information
  • Information submitted through forms, tickets, or bot commands

R8K0 Guardian does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, account login credentials, full payment card details, or other sensitive authentication information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide bot commands and features
  • Store server settings
  • Manage support tickets
  • Provide moderation and security features
  • Maintain server logs
  • Determine access to Free, Plus, or Premium features
  • Prevent abuse
  • Troubleshoot technical issues
  • Improve R8K0 Guardian

3. Discord Data

R8K0 Guardian communicates with Discord through Discord's API. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

R8K0 Mods is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases, configuration files, or other storage systems so R8K0 Guardian can continue functioning after restarts or outages.

R8K0 Mods aims to retain only information reasonably necessary to operate the bot and provide its features.

5. Data Sharing

R8K0 Mods does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate hosting or infrastructure services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Protect users, communities, or R8K0 Mods

6. Payment Information

If R8K0 Guardian offers paid subscriptions, payments may be processed by Discord or another authorized payment provider.

R8K0 Guardian does not directly store full credit card or debit card information.

7. Data Removal Requests

Users and server owners may request deletion of stored information associated with them or their Discord server.

Requests may be submitted through the official R8K0 Mods support Discord.

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

8. Children's Privacy

R8K0 Guardian is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as R8K0 Guardian changes. The most recent version will remain available on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact R8K0 Mods through the official R8K0 Mods support Discord server.
